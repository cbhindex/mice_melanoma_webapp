Group 1 — WM2664 (BRAF-inhibitor sensitive)

Interactive exploration of UNI-v2 (UNI2-h) tile embeddings from four H&E whole-slide images: two Vehicle mice (366, 364) and two PLX-4720 mice (365, 363). One slide per mouse; 21,113 tiles at 20× / 256 px.

⚠ With n = 2 mice per arm every comparison is preliminary and hypothesis-generating — look for candidate signals, treat apparent arm differences cautiously (they may be driven by a single mouse).

Embedding scatter →

UMAP / t-SNE of all tiles. Hover any dot to see the tile image and its metadata. Colour by treatment arm or by morphological cluster.

Tile similarity →

Click a tile to retrieve its most similar tiles across all slides (cosine similarity of UNI-v2 embeddings).

Cell map →

Nucleus-level UMAP/t-SNE (1 dot = 1 cell, CellViT). Hover a cell for its crop and metadata; its location lights up on the per-mouse cell-class maps.

Cell stats →

Cell-type proportions, by-class morphology & H&E stain, and nuclear density — interactive, with a 3-class ⇄ 5-class toggle.
