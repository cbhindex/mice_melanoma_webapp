Group 1 — WM2664 (BRAF-inhibitor sensitive)

Interactive exploration of four H&E whole-slide images — UNI-v2 (UNI2-h) tile embeddings and CellViT++ cell-level analysis: two Vehicle mice (366, 364) and two PLX-4720 mice (365, 363). One slide per mouse; 21,113 tiles at 20× / 256 px; 1,784,296 nuclei.

⚠ With n = 2 mice per arm every comparison is preliminary and hypothesis-generating — look for candidate signals, treat apparent arm differences cautiously (they may be driven by a single mouse).

Tile embedding →

UMAP / t-SNE of all 21,113 tiles. Hover any dot to see the tile image and its metadata; its location lights up on the per-mouse architecture heatmaps. Colour by treatment arm or by morphological cluster.

Tile similarity →

Click a tile to retrieve its most similar tiles across all slides (cosine similarity of UNI-v2 embeddings).

Cell map →

Nucleus-level t-SNE / UMAP (1 dot = 1 cell, CellViT++; 3 classes). Hover a cell for its crop and metadata; its location lights up on the per-mouse cell-class maps. Colour by class, arm, or a morphometric / stain feature.

Cell stats →

Cell-type proportions, by-class morphology & H&E stain, and nuclear density — interactive panels for the 3 classes (Neoplastic / Inflammation+Connective / Other).
