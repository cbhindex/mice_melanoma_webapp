What tissue environments are present—and do their neoplastic nuclei look different?

The image phenotypes are used as spatial context, not as diagnoses. This page asks what each phenotype contains, whether its nuclear morphology recurs across mice, and whether morphology can distinguish contexts in a completely held-out mouse.

MotivationCell shape can differ because the surrounding tissue architecture differs.
QuestionWhat might each tile phenotype represent, and are its neoplastic nuclei morphologically distinct?
MethodAssign nuclei to tiles, summarise within tile and mouse, then validate a tile-level classifier by leaving one mouse out.
Interactive resultInspect phenotype cards, compare a metric across clusters and view held-out-mouse classification.
InterpretationConcordance supports useful stratification, but it does not prove pathological identity.
